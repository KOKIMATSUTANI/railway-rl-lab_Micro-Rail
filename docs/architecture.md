## GTFS–OSM Integration (13.Aug.2026)

### Problem
GTFS and OSM do not share a reliable common identifier, and the correctness of proximity-based spatial matching is difficult to validate.

### Candidate
Evaluate a recent GTFS–OSM integration method with a public implementation.
Due to insufficient local computational resources, run the implementation on AWS using Terraform.

### Paper
Juri Penell, and Damian Dailisan (2026). "GROSS: German Rail Open-Source SUMO Scenario". Published at SUMO Conference 2026. Available at https://sumo.dlr.de/preprints/pre-print-3324.pdf
